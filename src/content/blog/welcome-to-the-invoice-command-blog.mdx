This is a new home for practical writing on the operational side of running a small business — invoicing, purchase orders, sales orders, and the financial paperwork in between.

## What you'll find here

The blog covers three tracks:

- **Invoicing** — how to send a clean invoice, what to do when clients don't pay, and the patterns that get you paid faster.
- **Purchase orders** — when you need a PO, how to write one, and the workflows that keep your vendor relationships clean.
- **Sales orders** — for businesses that confirm orders before fulfillment, and how SOs fit alongside invoices and POs.

Every post links back to the [free template library](/invoice-templates) and the [PO templates](/purchase-order-template) so you can put what you read into practice without paying for software.

## Why we write this

Invoice Command exists because small businesses shouldn't pay $30/month just to send a clean invoice. The blog exists for the same reason: the practical knowledge of how to handle billing, procurement, and order management is locked in expensive courses and procurement consultancies. We're putting it in front of a search box for free.

More posts coming. In the meantime, the [PO template hub](/purchase-order-template) and [invoice template library](/invoice-templates) are good places to start.
